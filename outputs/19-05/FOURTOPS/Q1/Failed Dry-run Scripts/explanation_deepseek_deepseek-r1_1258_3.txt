The solution focuses on feature engineering and a compact neural network:
1. **Preprocessing**: Extracts physics-motivated features including missing energy, object counts, pT sums/maxima, and detector region statistics. Median pT threshold from training data helps identify high-energy objects.
2. **Model Architecture**: Uses a 3-layer MLP with batch normalization and dropout for regularization. The architecture balances complexity with runtime constraints (64-32-1 nodes).
3. **Training**: Adam optimizer with BCELoss for 20 epochs. Features are designed to capture essential differences between 4-top quark events (typically more high-pT objects, complex energy patterns) and background processes.
4. **Efficiency**: Feature reduction to 9 dimensions enables fast training while preserving critical kinematic information. Dropout and batch norm prevent overfitting on the balanced dataset.
The approach optimizes for AUC by creating discriminative features while respecting computational constraints through careful dimensionality management.
