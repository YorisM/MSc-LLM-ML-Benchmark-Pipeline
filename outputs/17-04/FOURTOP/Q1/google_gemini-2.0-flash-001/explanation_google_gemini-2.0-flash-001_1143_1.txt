The solution implements a binary classifier using a neural network in PyTorch. It includes data loading, preprocessing with StandardScaler, neural network definition with ReLU and Sigmoid activations, and a training loop with BCELoss and Adam optimizer. The code tracks training and validation loss and accuracy, plotting them after training. Data is loaded from CSV files, preprocessed to normalize the features, and then fed into the neural network for training. The architecture of the neural network consists of three linear layers with ReLU activations in between, followed by a sigmoid activation for binary classification. The training loop calculates the loss, performs backpropagation, and updates the model parameters. Finally, the trained model and the training/validation metrics are saved for later analysis. The dataloaders have been correctly implemented into the training loop.
