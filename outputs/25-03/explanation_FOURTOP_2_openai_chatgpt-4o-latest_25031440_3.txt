### Data Preprocessing:
- The input data is normalized to facilitate training.
- Padding values (zeros) are handled through a masking mechanism to prevent spurious learning from non-existing objects.

### Model Architecture:
- A fully-connected deep neural network is selected due to the high-dimensional nature of input features.
- Batch normalization improves training stability and helps generalization.
- Dropout layers prevent overfitting by randomly dropping neurons during training.
- The final activation is a sigmoid function for binary classification.

### Training:
- Binary cross-entropy loss is used since this is a binary classification problem.
- The Adam optimizer is chosen for its adaptability and robustness.
- The model iterates over the training dataset in mini-batches to optimize computational efficiency.
- A validation step computes the AUC after every epoch to track model progress.

### Physics-Inspired Enhancements:
- The model accounts for missing transverse energy (E_T_miss), which plays a crucial role in distinguishing signal from background.
- Masking prevents fictitious correlations from artificially padded zeros, improving robustness.
- Regularization (dropout & weight decay) mitigates overfitting, especially crucial for high-energy physics datasets that have intrinsic noise.

### Expected Performance:
- By properly engineering features and training techniques, this approach aims to maximize the AUC, ideally achieving strong discrimination between four-top events and background processes.
